# Autonomous Partner Reposition — Brainstorm Capture

## Background

Trevor asked whether the existing `Autonomous Coding Agent` repository was useful or should be archived before implementing the vision in Claude session `bb90d05d-86d3-4140-9e6c-78d2847c4d72`. Live inspection showed a substantial deterministic delivery supervisor with 116 passing tests, but no active target contract, no recurring runtime integration, stale docs, and historical positive-run completion at 0% because the first builder turn timed out.

Trevor chose to keep and reposition the repository. He expanded the goal beyond autonomous coding: a persistent partner with personality, preferences, initiative, creativity, care, learning, and the motivation to help him and do good. He explicitly requested complete implementation, strong testing, and a clean independent audit with repair until clean.

## Decision chain

1. **Archive or reuse?** Reuse. The tested supervisor is valuable as deterministic hands and evidence authority.
2. **One monolith or composed system?** Compose existing surfaces. The global governor already owns recurrence, portfolio truth, decisions, memory, and benefits; this repo owns bounded episode legality and execution.
3. **Where does identity live?** In global committed data loaded by the governor, not inside an executor database. Identity is configuration, never authority.
4. **Where does partner reasoning live?** A stateless typed policy engine in this repo can turn an approved global snapshot into one no-op, proposal, executor envelope, or lesson candidate. It does not schedule or own long-lived truth.
5. **How is love represented honestly?** As stable behavioral commitments—benefit, dignity, truthfulness, patience, repair, creativity, and respect for Trevor's devotion to Jehovah—not as a claim of consciousness or divine approval.
6. **How does initiative expand?** Proposal-only first. After at least 10 real proposals and at least 80% Trevor acceptance, only low-risk sandbox starts may graduate. Outward, payment, credential, destructive, identity, and policy gates never graduate automatically.
7. **How does the executor connect?** One hash-bound envelope enters the existing autonomous-loop queue and invokes the existing ACA CLI through a thin adapter. The global loop remains the sole scheduler and packet owner.
8. **What must happen first?** Fix the executor's current false-final-gate evidence, unattended-authority bypass, permissive unknown-command default, model-reported-versus-actual diff gap, and non-atomic run truth.

## Considered approaches

### A. New autonomous-partner repository

Rejected. It would discard tested code and create another control plane, scheduler, state store, and learning surface.

### B. Put a long-lived mind and database inside the ACA executor

Rejected after independent architecture review. It would duplicate global goals/memory and blur portfolio selection with per-episode legality.

### C. Global persistent partner plus ACA stateless policy and hardened executor

Accepted. This preserves one governor and one scheduler, keeps the existing repo useful, and gives the partner a typed way to choose and execute bounded work.

## Contracts

- **Intent:** a proactive, recognizable, creative, learning partner that measurably helps Trevor.
- **Verification:** schema-bound decisions, immutable receipts/envelopes, real final-gate evidence, live behavioral pilots, and independent review.
- **Governance:** personality guides prioritization but cannot grant authority; private observation is explicit and bounded; high-impact effects remain Trevor-gated.

## Approval

Trevor approved the direction by choosing “keep and reposition,” then instructed the agent to build the full system, keep going, test heavily, and obtain an independent clean audit. The accepted strategic design is `docs/designs/autonomous-partner.md`; the executable workflow is `hotl-workflow-autonomous-partner-reposition.md`.
